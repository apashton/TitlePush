Play v0.6.8

Also as CHANGELOG.md and changelog.json.

v0.6.8 · What the coach thinks would have happened

20 Nov 2026, 12:12 pm UK · build 6edd270

  • After a match, your assistant may tell you what would have happened with one thing different: the style or formation that beats theirs, the half-time call you didn’t make, or (in a match you watch) the shout. It’s worked out by playing the same match again that way.
  • He’s honest about it: “we’d have won that” only when the replay was clearly better, “might have, could be luck” when it wasn’t, and when nothing would have saved it, he says so.
  • For a match you skip, the line appears with the last result on the main menu.

v0.6.7 · Counting visits to the website

20 Nov 2026, 11:42 am UK · build d74fc86

  • The website now counts visits, anonymously: no cookies, nothing about you, and nothing at all if your browser asks not to be tracked. The game opened from your own computer never counts.

v0.6.6 · Let the club build the ground

20 Nov 2026, 11:14 am UK · build d0feffb

  • Let the club build your ground. On the Ground page, or when you start a career, choose who builds it: you, or the club. The club builds what the next grade needs first, then what the fans are short of, sets fair prices and never spends you into the red. It pays a contractor 15% on top and won’t squeeze the most out of a sell-out, so a ground you design yourself can earn more.

v0.6.5 · Shouts without stopping the match

19 Nov 2026, 11:52 pm UK · build 169a156

  • Touchline shouts no longer stop the match. At 60′ and 75′ the shout pops up over the pitch while play goes on, and the clock slows down while it’s open so you have time to choose.
  • A shout takes effect from the end of the five minutes being played. Press nothing and the team carries on as it is.
  • Fixed: the match showed “Full time” at 60′ and 75′.

v0.6.4 · Matchday sound and cloud saves

19 Nov 2026, 11:09 pm UK · build b4da6ff

  • Matchday sound. The crowd murmurs, rises as the ball nears the box, roars at a goal and groans at a miss; you hear the ball, the net, the post and the referee’s whistle, and the rain when it’s raining. It’s all made by the game itself. Turn it off with the Sound switch by the match controls.
  • The training on the title screen and the hub joins the session part-way through, at a different drill each time, so it isn’t always the warm-up laps.
  • Cloud saves. Sign in with an email and password, or with Google, under Club → Save, and your career goes online: an autosave at the end of each week, and three slots of your own.
  • Carry on in another browser. Sign in on the title screen and load your career from the cloud.
  • The cloud never overwrites something you haven’t seen. If your cloud autosave came from another device, or is a different career, the game asks before replacing it.
  • The game still saves on your device, signed in or not, and plays offline.

v0.6.3 · Expected points, graded grounds and What’s New

19 Nov 2026, 10:11 pm UK · build 146e1c9

  • Expected points in the season review. Every match now keeps its expected goals, and the review says how many points your chances were worth against the points you got.
  • Other clubs’ grounds are graded by the same rules as yours. A club that hasn’t built what the tier above needs can’t go up, and the League table says what its ground is missing.
  • What’s New. This panel opens once after each new playtest, with the version history underneath. Open it again from the title screen or the footer.
  • The title screen and the footer show which playtest version you’re playing, and when it was built.

v0.6.2 · League trends

19 Nov 2026, 7:14 pm UK · build ba9ac3f

  • League trends on the Tactics screen. A new card shows which styles and formations the league is playing this season, with the top three of each and how often each one is used.
  • A headline when one style dominates. When one style is in at least 30% of game plans, you’ll see something like “High Press is everywhere this season”. Otherwise it says “No style dominates”.
  • How to beat the trend. The card names what counters the most common style and formation, for example “Counter or Long Ball beat High Press”.
  • No trend early in the season. It waits until about two rounds have been played before calling one.
  • Results are unchanged. The card only shows information, so no results or match numbers changed.

v0.6.1 · The formation cycle

19 Nov 2026, 6:14 pm UK · build 47a28dd

  • Formation rock-paper-scissors cycle: wide forwards beat midfield 4/5, midfield 4/5 beats back 3/5, and back 3/5 beats wide forwards.
  • The Tactics screen, opponent habits, the match report’s “Next time” tip, and half-time notes explain the cycle.

v0.6.0 · The first public playtest

19 Nov 2026, 4:58 pm UK · build 715cd03

  • The game is called Title Push.
  • A match report at full time: what went right, what went wrong, how lucky the finishing was, and a “Next time” tip.
  • Opponent habits on the Tactics screen: what this week’s opponent usually plays, how to counter it, and how your shape lines up against theirs.
  • Half-time choices: take the coach’s advice, stick with your own plan, or set orders for each kind of score and let the coach call it.
  • Touchline shouts at 60 and 75 minutes, to push for a goal or shut the game down.
  • A plan-book editor, and a line saying which plan this week’s match will use.
  • Away grounds drawn to the size of the home club’s ground.
  • Club → Save → “Export playtest timings” records how long you spent on each screen, to send with your feedback.

v0.5.0 · Matchday crowds and the season wrap

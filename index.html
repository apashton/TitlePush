Get your club to the top of the table.

Start on a roped-off park pitch in the County Sunday League, ten tiers from the top. Build the ground a stand at a time, outthink the manager in the other dugout, and push for the title every season until there's nowhere left to climb.

Play now

Free · plays in your browser · saves on your device · a playtest build

Build the ground

Terraces, seats, roofs, tea huts and turnstiles. Fans walk to their place, queue at half-time and judge the pies. A bigger, better ground pays for the next step up.

Read the game

Five simple dials and a grid for your players. Every style beats two and loses to two. Spot a manager's habits, counter them, shout from the touchline, and see what went right or wrong after the whistle.

Push for the title

Every league has a title. Win it at the bottom and you go up; win it at the top and it's the point. Twenty seasons to get as far as you can, with friends' leagues to race in later.

It's free: no adverts, and nothing in the game is for sale.
